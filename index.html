About关于我

I am a Biomedical Multimodal Large Model Scientist at the International Academy of Phronesis Medicine (Guangdong), where I also serve as a Principal Investigator (PI). I founded πAI Lab to pursue autonomous scientific discovery.我现任广东智慧医学国际研究院生物医学多模态大模型科学家，担任课题组负责人（PI），并创立 πAI Lab，探索自主科学发现

My training spans clinical medicine, medical imaging, pathology, and bioinformatics. I completed my doctorate at Peking Union Medical College (Tsinghua University School of Medicine).我的学术训练涵盖临床医学、医学影像、病理学与生物信息学。我于北京协和医学院（清华大学医学院）完成博士学业

Research directions研究方向

AI-ready Infrastructure and Autonomous DiscoveryAI-ready 基础设施与自主科学发现

Developing AI-ready scientific infrastructure and autonomous research agents that integrate knowledge, data, models, and tools to formulate, test, and refine scientific hypotheses构建 AI-ready 科学基础设施与自主研究智能体，整合知识、数据、模型与工具，贯通科学假设的提出、检验与迭代

AI Virtual Cells for Stromal Reprogramming面向基质重编程的 AI 虚拟细胞
